3 Run it

Watch & track him (recommended): searches the area around his city for his armies (farming or marching), then keeps following every one it found, dragging the map from one to the next. 4 Hunt his armies

Set the zoom once (recommended): zoom the game by hand to where you can still see the army icons but as much map as possible, then press the first button. Then zoom in until names show over armies and press the second. The bot then uses exactly those zooms every time (it zooms all the way out and scrolls back in the same number of notches) and learns the map directions at each.

Hunting sweeps the zoomed-out map around his city (wide view, armies show as grey diamonds), then zooms in on each new army just long enough to read the name over it, and follows the ones that are his. Before the first hunt, press Learn map directions once while zoomed out to the patrol level.
