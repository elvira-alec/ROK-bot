3 Run it

Open the game window, log in, go to the map and close any menus. Then press Start patrol. Leave both windows open.

4 Hunt his armies

Hunting sweeps the zoomed-out map around his city (wide view, armies show as grey diamonds), then zooms in on each new army just long enough to read the name over it, and follows the ones that are his. Before the first hunt, press Learn map directions once while zoomed out to the patrol level.
